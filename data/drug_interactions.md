# Drug Interactions

## Warfarin + Amiodarone
- **Effect:** Increased INR and bleeding risk; reduce warfarin dose by 30-50% and monitor INR closely
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Warfarin + Rifampin
- **Effect:** Decreased anticoagulant effect; increase monitoring and adjust warfarin dose
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Metformin + Furosemide
- **Effect:** May decrease metformin efficacy; monitor glucose
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Lisinopril + Spironolactone
- **Effect:** Risk of hyperkalemia; monitor potassium and renal function
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Sertraline + Tramadol
- **Effect:** Increased seizure risk and serotonin syndrome; avoid if possible
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Simvastatin + Clarithromycin
- **Effect:** Increased risk of rhabdomyolysis; contraindicated; switch to pravastatin or rosuvastatin
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Omeprazole + Clopidogrel
- **Effect:** Reduced antiplatelet effect of clopidogrel; consider pantoprazole instead
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Amlodipine + Simvastatin
- **Effect:** Increased simvastatin exposure; limit simvastatin to 20 mg/day
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Levothyroxine + Calcium Carbonate
- **Effect:** Decreased levothyroxine absorption; separate doses by ≥ 4 hours
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Fluconazole + Warfarin
- **Effect:** Enhanced anticoagulant effect; monitor INR more frequently
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Ibuprofen + Lisinopril
- **Effect:** Reduced antihypertensive effect and renal impairment risk
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Methotrexate + Ibuprofen
- **Effect:** Increased methotrexate toxicity; avoid NSAIDs
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Allopurinol + Azathioprine
- **Effect:** Increased azathioprine metabolite toxicity; reduce azathioprine dose to 25-33%
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Digoxin + Amiodarone
- **Effect:** Increased digoxin levels; reduce digoxin dose by 50% and monitor
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.

## Theophylline + Ciprofloxacin
- **Effect:** Increased theophylline levels and toxicity risk; monitor for seizures and arrhythmias
- **Management:** Monitor clinical status and adjust doses as needed. Consult pharmacy for complex regimens.
