# HealthTech Inc. – Employee Benefits Overview

**Version:** 2025 | **Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Owner:** Total Rewards, People Operations
**Access Tags:** all-employees

---

## 1. Health Insurance

HealthTech offers three medical plan options through Aetna:

| Plan | Type | Employee Premium/mo | Company Contribution |
|------|------|--------------------|--------------------|
| HealthTech Gold | PPO | $0 (employee only) | 100% premium (EE only) |
| HealthTech Silver | HDHP + HSA | $0 (employee only) | 100% premium (EE only) |
| HealthTech Bronze | HDHP | $0 (employee only) | 100% premium (EE only) |

**Dependent coverage:** HealthTech covers 80% of dependent premiums.

### 1.1 HSA Contributions (Silver Plan)

- Employer contribution: $1,200/year (single) / $2,400/year (family)
- 2025 IRS HSA limits: $4,150 (single) / $8,300 (family)

## 2. Dental & Vision

| Coverage | Plan | Employee Premium |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Dental | MetLife PPO | $0 (employee only); $30/mo family |
| Vision | EyeMed | $0 (employee only); $12/mo family |

- Dental annual max: $2,000 per covered person.
- Vision: $150 frame allowance + $150 contact lens allowance per year.

---

## 3. Life Insurance & Disability

| Benefit | Coverage | Cost |
|---------|---------|------|
| Basic Life | 2× annual salary (up to $500k) | Company-paid |
| Supplemental Life | Up to 5× salary (employee-elected) | Employee-paid |
| Short-Term Disability | 60% of salary for up to 12 weeks | Company-paid |
| Long-Term Disability | 60% of salary after 12 weeks (to age 65) | Company-paid |

---

## 4. Retirement (401k)

- **Plan:** Fidelity 401(k)
- **Employee contribution:** Up to IRS limit ($23,500 in 2025; $31,000 age 50+)
- **Company match:** 100% of first 4% of salary contributed
- **Vesting:** Company match is 100% immediately vested
- **Investment options:** 20+ Fidelity index funds + target-date funds

## 5. Paid Time Off

| Type | Annual Allocation |
|------|-----------------|
| PTO (vacation + personal) | 20 days/year (IC1–IC3); 25 days/year (IC4+) |
| Sick leave | Unlimited (notify manager before 10 AM) |
| Company holidays | 12 + 2 floating |
| Parental leave | 16 weeks (birthing); 8 weeks (non-birthing) |
| Bereavement | 5 days (immediate family); 3 days (extended family) |
| Jury duty | Full pay for up to 2 weeks |

PTO accrues from day 1 and has no carryover cap (US employees). Unused PTO is paid out upon
separation.

## 7. Mental Health Support

- **Spring Health EAP:** 8 free therapy sessions per year; 24/7 crisis line.
- **Calm app:** Full subscription company-paid.
- **No-questions-asked mental health days:** Up to 2 per year (separate from sick leave).

---

## 8. Other Perks

- **Equity:** All employees receive RSU grants (see Compensation Guidelines for details).
- **Commuter benefits:** Up to $315/month pre-tax for transit/parking (US).
- **Employee referral bonus:** $3,000 for successful engineering hires; $1,500 for other roles.
- **Pet insurance:** Voluntary plan via Figo; payroll deducted.

---

## 9. Benefits Enrollment

- **New hires:** 30-day enrollment window from start date.
- **Open enrollment:** October 15 – November 15 each year (changes effective Jan 1).
- **Qualifying life events:** Marriage, birth, adoption, loss of other coverage (30-day window).

Enroll or make changes at [benefits.healthtech.io](https://benefits.healthtech.io).
For questions: benefits@healthtech.io or Slack #people-ops.
