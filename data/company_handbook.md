**Version:** 3.2 | **Effective Date:** January 1, 2025 | **Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Our Mission

HealthTech Inc. exists to make high-quality healthcare information accessible, actionable, and
equitable for every patient, provider, and payer. We build AI-powered products that reduce
diagnostic delays, eliminate administrative waste, and improve clinical outcomes.

## 3. Employment Policies

### 3.1 Equal Employment Opportunity

HealthTech Inc. is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race,
color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, sexual orientation,
gender identity, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.

### 3.2 Work Hours & Flexibility

- Standard hours: 9 AM – 5 PM local time, Monday–Friday.
- Flexible start/end times available with manager approval.
- Remote-first: employees may work from any location within their contracted country.
- Core collaboration hours (all employees): 11 AM – 3 PM ET.

### 3.3 Time Off

- **Paid Time Off (PTO):** See HR Benefits Overview for current accrual schedule.
- **Sick Leave:** Unlimited sick days; employees notify their manager before 10 AM.
- **Company Holidays:** 12 observed US holidays + 2 floating holidays per year.
- **Parental Leave:** 16 weeks fully paid (birthing parent) / 8 weeks (non-birthing parent).

### 3.4 Code of Conduct

All employees must uphold the following standards at all times:
1. Treat every colleague, customer, and partner with respect and dignity.
2. Protect confidential company and patient information.
3. Report conflicts of interest to your manager and People Ops.
4. Never accept gifts valued above $50 from vendors without prior approval.
5. Use company resources for legitimate business purposes only.

---

## 4. IT & Data Security

Employees are required to:
- Use company-issued devices for all work involving patient data.
- Enable full-disk encryption and screen lock.
- Complete annual security awareness training.
- Report suspected phishing or security incidents within 1 hour to security@healthtech.io.

Refer to the **IT Security Policy** document for complete requirements.

---

## 5. Expense Reimbursement

Expenses under $50 may be submitted directly in Expensify with a receipt.
Expenses $50–$500 require manager pre-approval.
Expenses above $500 require VP-level pre-approval.
All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurring them.

---

## 6. Performance Reviews

HealthTech uses a bi-annual review cycle (June and December). Reviews assess:
- Goal achievement (OKRs)
- Core competency ratings
- 360-degree feedback from peers and cross-functional partners

Promotions and compensation changes are typically processed following the December review.

---

## 7. Reporting Concerns

Employees who witness potential violations of policy, law, or ethics should:
1. Speak directly with their manager (if appropriate).
2. Contact People Operations at peopleops@healthtech.io.
3. Use the anonymous Ethics Hotline: 1-800-ETHICS-1.

Retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited.
