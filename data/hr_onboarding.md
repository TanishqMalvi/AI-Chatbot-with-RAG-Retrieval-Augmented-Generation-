# HealthTech Inc. – Employee Onboarding Guide

**Version:** 2025-Q1 | **Owner:** People Operations
**Access Tags:** all-employees

---

## Welcome to HealthTech!

We're thrilled to have you on the team. This guide walks you through everything you need to
do in your first 30 days to get set up, connected, and contributing.

---

## Week 1: Getting Set Up

### Day 1 – IT Setup

1. **Collect your laptop** from the IT desk (or confirm shipment if remote).
2. **Activate your HealthTech Google Workspace account** using the credentials emailed to your
   personal address.
3. **Enroll in MFA** (Google Authenticator or YubiKey) – mandatory before accessing any system.
4. **Install required software:**
   - Slack (company communications)
   - Zoom (video conferencing)
   - 1Password (password manager – your vault will be pre-seeded)
   - Okta Verify (SSO gateway)
5. **Set up VPN:** Download Tailscale from the IT portal and authenticate with your Okta account.

### Day 1 – People Ops

- Sign your offer letter and PIIA (Proprietary Information & Inventions Agreement) in DocuSign.
- Complete I-9 verification with your People Ops buddy.
- Submit your direct deposit details in Rippling (payroll system).
- Enroll in benefits at [benefits.healthtech.io](https://benefits.healthtech.io) – you have
  30 days from start date to elect coverage.

### Day 1 – Meet Your Team

- Your manager will introduce you in your team Slack channel.
- Your onboarding buddy will reach out to schedule a 30-min intro coffee chat.
- Join the #new-hires Slack channel for peer support.

## Week 1–2: Mandatory Training

Complete the following within your first 10 business days:

| Training | Platform | Est. Time |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Security Awareness Fundamentals | KnowBe4 | 90 min |
| HIPAA Privacy & Security for HealthTech | Workramp | 60 min |
| Code of Conduct & Ethics | Workramp | 30 min |
| Data Classification & Handling | Workramp | 45 min |
| Incident Response Basics | Workramp | 30 min |

Your manager will verify completion. Failure to complete training within 10 days may restrict
your system access.

---

## Weeks 2–4: Role Ramp-Up

### Meetings to Schedule

- **1:1 with your manager** (weekly – your manager will set up a recurring invite)
- **Team standup** (daily or async – confirm cadence with your team)
- **Skip-level 1:1** with your manager's manager (end of week 4)
- **Cross-functional intros:** Schedule 30-min coffee chats with at least 3 people outside your team

### Key Systems Access

| System | Purpose | Access Method |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| GitHub | Code repository | SSO via Okta; request team repo access in #eng-access |
| Jira | Project management | SSO via Okta |
| Confluence | Internal wiki | SSO via Okta |
| AWS Console | Cloud infrastructure | Request IAM role via IT ticket |
| Datadog | Observability | SSO via Okta |
| Snowflake | Data warehouse | Request via #data-access |

## 30-Day Checkpoint

At the end of your first month, complete a 30-day check-in with your manager:
- Review initial OKRs / goals
- Confirm all training is complete
- Discuss any blockers or open questions
- Complete the new-hire feedback survey (link emailed by People Ops)
