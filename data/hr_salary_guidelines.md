## 1. Compensation Philosophy

HealthTech Inc. targets compensation at the **60th percentile** of the market for each role
band, using Radford Global Technology Survey and Levels.fyi as primary benchmarks.
Total compensation includes:
- Base salary
- Annual performance bonus (10–20% of base)
- Equity (RSUs, 4-year vest, 1-year cliff)
- Benefits (health, dental, vision, 401k match up to 4%)

---

## 5. Equity

- All full-time employees receive RSU grants at hire.
- Grant size is determined by level and annual grant refresh budget.
- Vesting: 4-year schedule with 1-year cliff (25% at year 1, then monthly).
- Refresh grants are issued annually at Board discretion.

---

## 6. Merit Increase Process

1. Managers submit merit recommendations in the HR portal by December 15.
2. Increases must keep employees within their band (exceptions require VP approval).
3. Typical merit range: 2–6% for "meets expectations"; 6–10% for "exceeds expectations".
4. Off-cycle increases require written justification and CHRO sign-off.

---

## 7. Compensation Review Request Process

Employees who believe their compensation is out-of-band may submit a formal review request to
peopleops@healthtech.io. Requests are reviewed quarterly. Outcomes are communicated within
30 business days.
